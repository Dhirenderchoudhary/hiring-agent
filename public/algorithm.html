How a score is made

The PDF is read, then marked. Nothing runs until you press Run analysis.

Rubric

Open source · 35

Other people’s public projects. Your own repos alone stay at 10 or under.

Self projects · 30

Things you built. Live URLs and real users count. Tutorial clones are marked down.

Production · 25

Shipped work: a job, an internship, or a product other people run.

Technical skills · 10

The stack counts when it shows up in the work, not as a list by itself.

Adjustments

Bonus, up to 20

Portfolio, LinkedIn, a technical blog, Google Summer of Code, GirlScript Summer of Code, founder or early-startup roles.

Deductions

Tutorial-only projects, generic names, and broken or missing links.

Ignored

Name, college, grades, and city. A second run can move a point because a model writes the evidence.

The file

No account. The PDF is deleted after scoring. A GitHub link on the resume is read as a public profile only.
